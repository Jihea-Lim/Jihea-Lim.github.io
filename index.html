Jihea Lim

Hi! I am a PhD student at Incheon Nation University, supervised by Prof.Gwanggil Jeon and Prof.Young-Sup Lee. I received my BEng degree in Embedded Systems Engineering from Incheon Nation University, where I am currently pursuing my PhD degree.
My research focuses on learning-based active noise control (ANC) using reinforcement learning (RL) and deep learning (DL), particularly ANC agents that learn through interaction with the physical world.

Feel free to reach out to me if you'd like to discuss research or explore potential collaboration!
